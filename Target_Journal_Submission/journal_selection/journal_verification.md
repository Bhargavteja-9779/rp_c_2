# Verification log for GENETICS (performed 2026-10-03)

## 1. Legitimacy and integrity
* GENETICS has been published since 1916 as the flagship journal of the Genetics Society of America, a scientific society. It is published by Oxford University Press, a university press.
* It is not on any predatory or hijacked-journal list we know of. Its domain (academic.oup.com/genetics) and DOI prefix (10.1093/genetics) are consistent with the Crossref and PubMed records checked below.
* Review is single-anonymized. GSA policies cover data release, image integrity, authorship, and disclosure of AI tool use.
* We found no publication-integrity concern (such as a Clarivate suppression or mass retraction) for this journal.

## 3. Submission-to-acceptance time
* Data source: NCBI E-utilities, PubMed `History/PubMedPubDate[@PubStatus='received'|'accepted']`.
* Cohort: journal articles in "Genetics"[jour] with received date 2025-01-01 to 2025-06-30 that had been published by the query date. Result: n = 137, median **69 days**, P25 41, P75 112. Shares: ≤72 days 54.7%, ≤60 days 40.9%, ≤30 days 10.9%.
* Sample of articles published 2025–2026 (all received years): n = 493, median 68 days, ≤72 days 54.6%.
* Caveats:
  * Dates are as deposited by the publisher.
  * Only accepted papers are included.
  * The cohort can still be right-censored for papers from that window that are under very long revision and not yet published. Any such censoring makes the true median slightly longer.
* We did not find any official GENETICS statement of first-decision or acceptance time. The figure above is a **measured submission-to-acceptance** time, not a first-decision time.

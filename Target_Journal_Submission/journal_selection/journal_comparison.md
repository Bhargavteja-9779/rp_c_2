Hard constraints: JCR-indexed; Q1 preferred (Q2 acceptable only when time ≤72 days); reputable publisher; no MDPI; median submission-to-acceptance **≤72 days**; genuine scope fit.

**Timeline method.** S→A means submission-to-acceptance, computed from public article-history dates. Most rows use PubMed `received`/`accepted` dates. Rows marked (CR) use Crossref "assertion" dates deposited by the publisher. Rows marked "cohort" cover articles **received** Jan–Jun 2025: these are less biased, because fast papers are not over-represented. Rows marked "recent" sample about 300 articles published 2025–26 and are slightly biased towards fast papers. These are our own measurements, not publisher statements.

**Decision rationale.** GENETICS is the only journal in the screen that meets all of the following at once:
* Q1 in the latest JCR,
* a high JIF for its field (6.5),
* a measured median submission-to-acceptance of ≤72 days (69 days),
* a reputable, non-commercial society publisher (GSA with OUP, matching the stated "Oxford-type" preference),
* strong methodological scope fit.

Frontiers in Plant Science is faster on the biased "recent" sample, but has a lower JIF, a narrower scope and a borderline cohort estimate.
